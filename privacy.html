Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

This policy explains what information Thoughtful 360 ("we", "us") handles through this website, our social media channels, and the Thoughtful 360 publishing app we use to manage our own YouTube channel. In short: we don't collect personal information from visitors, and our app only ever accesses our own account.

1. This website

  • The site is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages.
  • We do not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or forms, and we do not ask you for any personal information.
  • Like any web host, GitHub may record technical data such as your IP address in its server logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Pages load the Poppins font from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver the font. See the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Our social media channels

When you watch, like, comment on or follow Thoughtful 360 on YouTube, Instagram or Facebook, that platform processes your data under its own privacy policy. We only see what those platforms show any channel owner, such as public comments and aggregated, anonymous viewing statistics.

3. The Thoughtful 360 publishing app (YouTube API Services)

We use a private tool, "Thoughtful 360", that connects to YouTube through the YouTube API Services so we can publish and manage videos on our own channel.

  • Who uses it: only the owner of the Thoughtful 360 channel. It is not offered to the public, and no other person's Google account is connected to it.
  • What it accesses: with the owner's permission, it uploads videos to the Thoughtful 360 channel, reads that channel's basic details, and manages that channel's own videos, playlists and comments.
  • What it stores: an access token for our own channel, kept privately on the owner's computer. It does not collect, store or share any data about viewers or other YouTube users.
  • Sharing: we do not sell, rent or share any data obtained through the YouTube API Services with anyone.

By using YouTube you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access granted to the app can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page.

4. Email

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply to you. We don't add you to any mailing list and we don't share your email.

5. Children

Our content and website are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy: thoughtful360x@gmail.com
